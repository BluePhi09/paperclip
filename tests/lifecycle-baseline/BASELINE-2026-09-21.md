## Observed differences

- **36 legacy narrative pairs:** holding structured state constant and changing
  wording in summaries, results, comments, continuation summaries, stdout or
  stderr changes continuation effects. Examples include negation, quoted history,
  Spanish, an unsupported completion claim and empty output. The test compares
  enqueue/skip/exhaustion and deduplication identity, not just diagnostic labels.
- **4 legacy work-mode probes:** `report`, `plan` or `research` in a title, or
  `report` in a description, changes continuation admission without a structured
  work-mode change.
- **1 commentary/progress probe:** one new issue comment changes classification
  from `plan_only` to `advanced` and the continuation decision from `exhausted` to
  `skip`. The recorded attempt remains 2; this observation does **not** establish
  that the numeric retry counter resets.
- **1 legacy heartbeat pair:** both variants finish in two provider turns, but
  adding “No approval required” changes the persisted follow-up path from
  `run_liveness_continuation` to `finish_successful_run_handoff`.
- **2 native continuation probes:** the two-step and longer productive workflow
  each stop after one successful provider turn. The task remains `in_progress`,
  its execution lock is clear, and continuation wakes are coalesced after another
  explicit native-wake dispatch and queue drain. This establishes the bounded
  observation only. It does not establish permanent stalling, the outcome of a
  later recovery sweep, or a separate failure-budget defect in the longer case.

**Attribution correction after inspecting the live tool boundary:** these two
native probes inject an internal `same_agent` compatibility result. The current
model-facing completion schema accepts `response_wake` instead. Their observations
are retained, but they are not evidence of a reachable current real-LLM
continuation defect. See the [live suite and remaining boundaries](../runner-e2e/LIFECYCLE-BASELINE.md).

The 44 failed assertions are repeated probes of these behaviors, not 44 distinct
product defects. No lifecycle policy or production implementation was changed.
